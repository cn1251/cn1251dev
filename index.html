About

Cassandra Noorman received her Master's degree in Chemistry from the University of Nottingham in 2026, having joined the Ermanis research group and completed her project on identifying novel nonclassical carbanions using DFT. She was subsequently admitted as an Associate Member of the Royal Society of Chemistry, and is now pursuing a career in education; teaching chemistry in secondary academies in England while studying for a Postgraduate Certificate in Education with the University of Derby.

For more information on her research, visit the Ermanis Group website.

Cassandra also has a strong interest in open source development, and is looking to join and contribute to open source projects in the future.
